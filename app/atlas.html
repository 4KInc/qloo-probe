Audiences, discovered — not assumed

We did not pick these groups. Each one is a set of artists, shows and games whose film-affinity signatures correlate, found by clustering the taste graph. Distance on this map is taste distance, not geography.

Clusters are joined by average linkage at correlation 0.75, the tightness measured to survive resampling of the film set; looser grouping produced one large cluster that dissolved under the same test. These are communities of film affinity as Qloo models it, not groups of people, and anchors that joined no cluster are left off the map rather than forced into one. The map is precomputed, so opening it spends no Qloo calls.
