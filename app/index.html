Travels Well

Which titles in a slate travel to another market, which do not, and which the evidence cannot settle. Every market is read twice through unrelated taste anchors; only what both readings agree on is reported.

Pick a slate to run.

Answers are labelled LIVE or CACHED; a cached answer is a recorded run of this same pipeline, never a synthetic stand-in. Where two readings of a market disagree about a title, it is withheld rather than ranked. Where a market is too thinly covered, or a baseline too unstable to measure against, the agent says so instead of guessing.
