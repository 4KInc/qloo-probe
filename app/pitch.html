Pitch Desk

Your slate · their catalogue · what to lead with

You have twenty minutes and five slots. Describe a buyer by what they have already licensed, and this ranks your own titles for them — measured against the audience your slate already plays to, because the titles that travel are rarely the ones you love at home.

A buyer's catalogue of six titles or more is split into halves, each half read independently, and any of your titles the two halves rank very differently is withheld rather than reported. Titles appearing in both your slate and a describing catalogue are removed and named: Qloo excludes an entity from its own results, so they cannot be scored against themselves.
